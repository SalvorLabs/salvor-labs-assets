## Editorial sequence

The operator clarified on 20 October that the first announcement should reveal only the high-level direction of all five pillars. Keep mechanics, thresholds, economic parameters, and internal examples out of this release.

A more detailed follow-up is tentatively intended in about two weeks, around 3 November 2026, subject to readiness. This supersedes the source brief's earlier “later in the week” timing for the copy pack. The later update can explain the five pillars' proposed mechanics once reviewed. This is an editorial target, not a public launch date, scheduled post, or promise of feature availability.

Use `$SALVOR` when referring to the token. Use “Salvor OS” for the free open-source foundation and “Salvor+” / “Salvor Plus” for the private commercial derivative. The operator further clarified that all five token-enabled services and programs belong under Salvor+, not the Salvor OS feature set. Bounties and builder benefits can support OS work and contributors while remaining Salvor+ programs. Every current public surface states that the new utility directions are not live offerings.
